OpSense — Agentic AI for Operational Drift Detection

A personal experimental project exploring OpenAI’s AgentKit concepts through a lightweight agentic system. OPSENSE uses dummy operational data to simulate real-world systems, allowing an autonomous AI agent to reason step by step, decide when to invoke tools, and converge to a final optimization report with explicit stopping conditions.

Designed as a foundational exploration of agentic AI, the architecture is intentionally versatile and transferable across domains such as fintech, healthtech, infrastructure, and large-scale platforms.
